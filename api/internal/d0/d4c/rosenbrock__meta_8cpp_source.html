33 "Rosenbrock-Wanner integrator for stiff ODEs and semi-explicit index-1\n"
34 " DAEs, with adaptive or fixed steps.\n"
35 "\n"
36 "An 8-stage, stiffly accurate, L-stable method of order 5 with an \n"
37 "embedded method of order 4. Each step needs one Jacobian and one \n"
38 "sparse QR factorization; the method is linearly implicit, without \n"
39 "Newton iterations. Steps cross the output times within a control \n"
40 "group, which are interpolated by a dense output of order 4. Step sizes\n"
41 " are chosen from the nominal solution only, so forward sensitivities \n"
42 "are the exact derivatives of the discrete solution (internal numerical\n"
43 " differentiation) and adjoint sensitivities are the exact discrete \n"
44 "adjoint of the taped steps. With step_size, each run of steps over a \n"
45 "control group is divided into equal steps instead, and the tape has a \n"
46 "known bound.\n"
47 "\n"
48 "Zero-crossing events (DAE output zero, option transition) are located \n"
49 "on the dense output of the step in which a function becomes negative; \n"
50 "integration restarts from the state after the transition. \n"
51 "Sensitivities, forward, adjoint and forward-over-adjoint, include \n"
52 "those of the event times.\n"
